# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Assume good faith, and accept that maintainers have the final say on what is merged.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's
"Report content" option or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that break these rules.
